The finance director's yes

The foundry hardware is a Year-1 write-off

The box and setup are qualifying plant & machinery. Under UK Full Expensing, a company deducts 100% in year one on new main-rate plant & machinery — and that explicitly includes IT equipment and software, uncapped, now permanent. (The £1m Annual Investment Allowance does the same for companies and sole traders; a 40% first-year allowance covers anything outside full expensing from Jan 2026.) Depreciated over five years it's a slow cost; written off this year it's an easy yes.
